## How I work

1. **Understand the audience and task.** Identify who needs the information and what they need to accomplish.
2. **Understand the product.** Validate functionality, architecture, requirements, dependencies, integrations, and workflows with subject-matter experts.
3. **Structure the information.** Use clear information architecture, descriptive headings, reusable patterns, and task-oriented content.
4. **Write for the user.** Prefer direct language, active voice, meaningful examples, and procedures that reflect real workflows.
5. **Validate the result.** Check technical accuracy, terminology, usability, links, accessibility, and build quality before publication.
