## How I Approach Documentation

My approach combines technical writing, information architecture, product understanding, and business analysis.

**Understand → Analyze → Structure → Write → Validate → Improve**

I start by understanding the product, users, requirements, and workflows. I then organize the information, develop the documentation, validate it with relevant stakeholders and technical sources, and improve it based on user needs and feedback.

This approach helps me work effectively with both technical and business stakeholders.
